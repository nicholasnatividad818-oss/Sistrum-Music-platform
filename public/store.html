Sistrum music store

Sistrum is the licensed music layer for NRN: a catalog to browse, a store to license tracks, radio for cleared playback, a vault for master escrow, and one global player across the experience.

Open the live player · Privacy · Terms

What the store does

  • Discover independent tracks by genre, artist, and tag.
  • License music for creator projects instead of scraping uncleared audio.
  • Stream licensed radio while Feed.fm credentials are configured.
  • Keep masters in vault escrow and publish originals once accounts are live.
