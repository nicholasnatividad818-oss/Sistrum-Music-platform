Privacy Policy

Last updated September 13, 2026. Sistrum is operated by NRN.

Sistrum stores playlists, uploads, and comments you create in this browser. Spotify tokens and your Spotify account id stay on this device after you connect. They are not sent to a Sistrum server today.

Licensed radio and clips are provided by Feed.fm / Feed Clips under their terms. Spotify catalog data stays on Spotify. On-chain vault activity is public on the network you use.
