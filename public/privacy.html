Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026. Sistrum is operated by NRN.

Sistrum stores playlists, uploads, and comments you create in this browser. Spotify tokens and your Spotify account id stay on this device after you connect. They are not sent to a Sistrum server today.

Licensed radio and clips are provided by Feed.fm / Feed Clips under their terms. Spotify catalog data stays on Spotify. On-chain vault activity is public on the network you use.

When a public PostHog project key is configured, Sistrum sends product analytics to PostHog: page views, the screen you open, track plays by id, and your account id after you sign in. Session replay is off. Sistrum does not send your email address to PostHog. Analytics stay off when that key is blank.

Contact: the NRN account that owns this deployment.
